Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Peach Parking Solutions ("Peach Parking," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide when you visit our website, request a quote, apply for employment, file a claim, or otherwise communicate with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website or services:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Event or service details — venue, date, service type, and related information submitted through our contact, quote, or claim forms.
  • Employment information — details submitted through our Join Our Team application, including work history and resume.
  • Claim information — vehicle details, incident descriptions, and photos submitted through our claims form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes for our services
  • Schedule and coordinate valet, parking management, and event parking services
  • Process and investigate claims
  • Review employment applications
  • Communicate with you about your request, including by phone, email, or text message (SMS)

SMS & Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number, Peach Parking Solutions may contact you by text message regarding your inquiry, quote, booking, or claim. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or reply STOP at any time to opt out of text communications. You can also contact us directly at (404) 345-2080 or help@peachparkingsolutions.com.

We Do Not Share Your Information

Peach Parking Solutions does not sell, rent, or share your personal information or phone number with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third party for marketing purposes, under any circumstance.

We may share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as scheduling, payment processing, or email/SMS delivery platforms), and only as needed to provide our services to you. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and are prohibited from using it for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Peach Parking Solutions, our clients, or others.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information you share with us from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to opt out of future communications.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
